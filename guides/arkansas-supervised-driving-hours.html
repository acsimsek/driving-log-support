Arkansas learner permit practice requirements

Verified against official Arkansas sources on 2026-07-31.

180 dayspermit holding period
Arkansas's cited graduated-licensing source does not set a numeric supervised-practice-hour minimum. The permit must still be held for 180 days. That makes the non-hour eligibility steps below especially important; an app total is a personal record, not a substitute for those state requirements.
