California 50-hour log: DL 603 and the permit signature

For the teen learner-permit path. Rule data verified 2026-09-21; linked paperwork checked 27 September 2026.

California requires 50 hours of supervised practice, including 10 at night. The DL 603 log is optional; the parent or guardian certifies completion by signing the instruction permit.

Which paper does what?

The DMV Parent-Teen Training Guide (DL 603) includes a practice log. Use it to organise dates and day/night time, or keep another accurate record. An app printout helps you total the practice; it does not replace the permit signature.

Before the driving test, hold the permit for at least 6 months and complete the practice. The parent or guardian signs the instruction permit to certify it. Follow the DMV application checklist for the remaining course, training and test requirements.

Mistakes that leave the paperwork unfinished

  • Taking a log printout but leaving the certification on the permit unsigned.
  • Reaching 50 total hours without 10 night hours.
  • Treating the hour target as permission to skip the 6-month holding period.
  • Calling an app-generated supporting record a DMV form.

Keep the running log during practice; check the permit signature and DMV checklist before the appointment. The log and the certification serve different purposes.
