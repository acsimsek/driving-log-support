How many supervised driving hours does California require?

California requires 50 hours of supervised practice, including 10 at night. On top of the total, the default supervised period is 6 calendar months, starting at permit issue date.

California's DL 603 driving log is optional — what the DMV actually requires is the parent or guardian certifying the 50 hours by signing the instruction permit itself. Many families keep a log anyway, because the certification is a legal statement and a dated record is what backs it up.
