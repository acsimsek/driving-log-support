How many supervised driving hours does Colorado require?

Colorado requires 50 or 62 hours of supervised practice, including 10 at night.

Checked against official Colorado sources on 2026-07-31.

Colorado has more than one practice-hour path. The standard target is 50 hours, while the qualifying path described below changes it to 62 hours. Record which path applies before relying on the lower or higher total.
