Delaware supervised driving hours

Verified against official Delaware sources on 2026-07-31.

50supervised hours required
10of those hours at night
180 dayspermit holding period
For Delaware, the practical finish line is not just recording 50 supervised hours, including 10 at night; it is being able to complete the state's proof or certification described below. Keep dates and day/night time as you go so the final paperwork is supportable.
