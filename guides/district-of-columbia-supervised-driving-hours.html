How many supervised driving hours does District of Columbia require?

District of Columbia requires 50 hours of supervised practice, including 10 at night. On top of the total, the default supervised period is 6 calendar months, starting at permit issue date.

District of Columbia splits supervised practice across licence stages rather than treating the 50-hour total as one undifferentiated bucket. The stage notes below matter because time may need to be completed before a particular application step.

Current licence-stage requirements

  • learner: 40 hours, including 0 at night.
  • intermediate: 10 hours, including 10 at night; 6 calendar months from the intermediate/provisional license issue date.

Stage-by-stage totals

  • The first 40 hours must be completed before applying for the provisional license.
  • 10 additional night hours completed after the provisional stage.

Which paperwork does District of Columbia want?

Certification of Eligibility for Provisional License (40 hours) + Certification of Eligibility for Full License with Conditions (10 night hours)
