How many supervised driving hours does Illinois require?

Illinois requires 50 hours of supervised practice, including 10 at night. On top of the total, the permit must be held for 9 months.

How long must the permit be held in Illinois?

At least 9 months. The clock runs from the date the permit was issued, not from the first logged drive.
