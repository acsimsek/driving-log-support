Iowa supervised driving hours

Verified against official Iowa sources on 2026-07-31.

30supervised hours required
4of those hours at night
Iowa splits supervised practice across licence stages rather than treating the 30-hour total as one undifferentiated bucket. The stage notes below matter because time may need to be completed before a particular application step.

The requirement at a glance

Supervised practice required30 hours
Of which at night4 hours
Proof/signature modelone final certification

Stage-by-stage totals

  • 20 hours before the intermediate license; 2 of them between sunset and sunrise.
  • 10 additional hours during the intermediate stage before the full license; 2 of them at night.

The paperwork

The certification on the Parent's/Guardian's Written Consent form
