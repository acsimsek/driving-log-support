Read the Kansas rules
The official Kansas paperwork is 50 Hour Affidavit signed by the parent or guardian; submitting a detailed log is not required. This sheet is not an official form: it is a dated record you can keep as you go and copy onto, or attach to, whatever Kansas asks for. Official source.
