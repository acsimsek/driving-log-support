How many supervised driving hours does Maryland require?

Maryland requires 60 hours of supervised practice, including 10 at night for the matching path. On top of the total, the default supervised period is 9 calendar months, starting at permit issue date. These are the default figures; age, permit date and course choices change the target as detailed below.

Choose the matching applicant path

  • Under 18, or age 18 without a diploma / equivalent: Hold the permit for 9 months; complete driver education and 60 hours, including 10 at night. Moving violations can extend eligibility.
  • Age 18 with a high-school diploma / equivalent: The diploma or equivalent reduces the permit period to 3 months; 60/10 practice and driver education remain required.
  • Age 19–24: Hold the permit for 3 months; complete 60 practice hours including 10 at night and approved driver education.
  • Age 25 or older: 45 days with the permit, 14 practice hours including 3 nighttime hours, plus driver education.
