How many supervised driving hours does Massachusetts require?

Massachusetts requires 30 or 40 hours of supervised practice. On top of the total, the default supervised period is 6 calendar months, starting at permit issue date.

Massachusetts has more than one practice-hour path. The standard target is 40 hours, while the qualifying path described below changes it to 30 hours. Record which path applies before relying on the lower or higher total.
