Massachusetts supervised driving hours

Verified against official Massachusetts sources on 2026-07-31.

30 or 40supervised hours required
180 dayspermit holding period
Massachusetts has more than one practice-hour path. The standard target is 40 hours, while the qualifying path described below changes it to 30 hours. Record which path applies before relying on the lower or higher total.

The requirement at a glance

Supervised practice required40 hours
If the applicant completes a driver skills development program30 hours
Permit must be held180 days
Supervising adultage 21+; licensed 1+ years
Proof/signature modelone final certification
Permit-stage limit: Permit holders under 18 may drive between 00:00 and 05:00 only with a parent or guardian

Also required in Massachusetts

  • Driver education: 30 hours classroom + 12 hours behind the wheel + 6 hours observation
  • The PARENT attends a 2-hour class (if not attended in the last 5 years)
  • 6 months with a clean driving record
