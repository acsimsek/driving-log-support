Michigan driving log: 30 hours for Segment 2, 50 for the road test

For the teen learner-permit path. Rule data verified 2026-09-21; linked paperwork checked 17 October 2026.

50supervised hours required
10of those hours at night
Michigan teens on a Level 1 license log supervised driving twice over: 30 hours, including 2 at night, before starting Segment 2, and 50 hours, including 10 at night, before the driving skills test. The parent or guardian signs the log, and it is shown to the Segment 2 instructor and again to the test examiner.

Two checkpoints on one log

CheckpointHours loggedAlso required
Before Segment 230 hours, 2 at nightLevel 1 license held 3 consecutive months; show the log to the Segment 2 instructor
Before the driving skills test50 hours, 10 at nightSegment 2 completed, Level 1 held 6 months, no violations in the previous 90 days; show the signed log to the examiner

The state's Michigan Supervised Driving Log (Rev. 08/2019) has columns for the date, lesson number, day hours, night hours, weather and the supervisor's initials. The Secretary of State says other log formats are accepted. At the skills test the parent or guardian also signs a certification on the score sheet.

One wrinkle: the form's Segment 2 block asks for "30 minimum" daytime hours and "2 minimum" nighttime hours, while the instructions and the statute say 30 hours including 2 at night. If your Segment 2 school reads it differently, ask them before the first class.

What counts as night in Michigan

The official log defines nighttime hours as driving from 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise. A drive that starts at sunset is not night practice for its first half hour.

Who can supervise

A licensed parent or guardian, or a licensed adult aged 21 or older whom the parent or guardian designates. The Secretary of State says the designated adult must carry a signed letter of authorization from the parent or guardian. The supervisor sits in the front seat.

Mistakes that hold up Segment 2 or the test

  • Arriving at Segment 2 with fewer than 30 logged hours or no night hours yet.
  • Counting the half hour right after sunset as night.
  • A grandparent or family friend supervising without the parent's signed letter.
  • Reaching 50 hours before the Level 1 license has been held for 6 months and booking the test too early.
  • A log with totals but no dates or initials; it is checked twice.
