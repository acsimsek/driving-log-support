How many supervised driving hours does Missouri require?

Missouri requires 40 hours of supervised practice, including 10 at night. On top of the total, the permit must be held for 182 days.

Common questions

How many driving hours do you need in Missouri?

Missouri requires 40 hours, including 10 at night.

What counts as night driving in Missouri?

Missouri defines it as between sunset and sunrise. Record the day and night parts of a drive separately as you go; splitting them afterwards is guesswork.

How long must the permit be held in Missouri?

At least 182 days, counted from the date the permit was issued.
