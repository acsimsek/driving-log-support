Nevada supervised driving hours

Verified against official Nevada sources on 2026-07-31.

50 or 100supervised hours required
10of those hours at night
Nevada's DLD130 log must be handwritten in blue or black ink with original signatures, and it splits every session into daytime and nighttime columns. Rural learners without reachable driver education have a different path: 100 hours instead of 50.
