How many supervised driving hours does New Hampshire require?

New Hampshire requires 40 hours of supervised practice, including 10 at night.

Checked against official New Hampshire sources on 2026-09-21.

For New Hampshire, the practical finish line is not just recording 40 supervised hours, including 10 at night; it is being able to complete the state's proof or certification described below. Keep dates and day/night time as you go so the final paperwork is supportable.

What counts as night driving in New Hampshire?

New Hampshire defines it as from 30 minutes after sunset to 30 minutes before sunrise. Record the day and night parts of a drive separately as you go; splitting them afterwards is guesswork.
