How many supervised driving hours does New Jersey require?

New Jersey requires 50 hours of supervised practice, including 10 at night. On top of the total, the default supervised period is 6 calendar months, starting at permit validation after the knowledge test. These are the default figures; age, permit date and course choices change the target as detailed below.

Choose the matching applicant path

  • Under 21; permit issued on or after February 1, 2025: Complete 50 hours including 10 during darkness, and six months with a validated permit.
  • Now 21+; permit issued under 21 on or after February 1, 2025: Turning 21 does not remove the 50/10-hour certification. The adult practice period is three months after permit validation.
  • Under 21; permit issued before February 1, 2025: The new 50-hour certification does not apply to a permit issued before February 1, 2025; the six-month supervised period remains.
  • Age 21+; permit issued at 21+ or before February 1, 2025: The 50-hour certification does not apply. Complete three months of supervised practice after permit validation.

New Jersey defines it as during darkness. Record the day and night parts of a drive separately as you go; splitting them afterwards is guesswork.
