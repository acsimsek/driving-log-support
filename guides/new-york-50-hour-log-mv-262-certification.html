New York 50-hour driving log: MV-262 and the hours after sunset

For the teen learner-permit path. Rule data verified 2026-09-21; linked paperwork checked 17 October 2026.

50supervised hours required
15of those hours at night
6 calendar monthsdefault supervised period
New York junior permit holders need 50 hours of supervised practice, at least 15 of them after sunset, and must wait 6 months from the permit date to schedule the road test. A parent or guardian certifies the hours on form MV-262, which goes to the examiner at every road test.

One form, two sections

The MV-262 Certification of Supervised Driving (revision 3/25) certifies totals; DMV does not ask for the log itself, and the form has no notary step. A parent or guardian signs it. Hours driven in a certified driver education course or with a driving school count toward the 50, and the school certifies those in its own section of the same form.

Bring the signed MV-262 to every road test, not only the first. Before the test you also need the 5-hour pre-licensing course certificate (MV-278) or a driver education completion certificate (MV-285) on file, as DMV's pre-licensing page explains. The form warns that false information on it may be a crime.

Who can supervise, and where

The supervisor must be at least 21 and hold a license valid in New York for that type of vehicle. New York also limits where and when permit holders drive (DMV's learner permit restrictions):

Region5 a.m. to 9 p.m.9 p.m. to 5 a.m.
New York CityOnly in a dual-control car, with a parent, guardian, driver education teacher or driving school instructorNo driving

For NYC and Long Island families this shapes the 15 night hours: they have to fall between sunset and 9 p.m. In June that window is about half an hour; from November to February it is several hours, so plan the after-sunset practice for the darker months.

Mistakes that delay the road test

  • Leaving the MV-262 at home for a second or third road test attempt.
  • Reaching 50 hours with fewer than 15 after sunset.
  • Planning NYC or Long Island night practice after 9 p.m., when permit holders may not drive at all.
  • Treating the Driver's Manual's suggested 10 hours in moderate to heavy traffic as the requirement. It is a recommendation; the certified numbers are 50 and 15.
  • Forgetting the MV-278 or MV-285 certificate. The MV-262 does not replace it.
