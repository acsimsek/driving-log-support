North Carolina 60-hour log: the weekly cap explained

For the teen learner-permit path. Rule data verified 2026-09-21; linked paperwork checked 27 September 2026.

60supervised hours required
10of those hours at night

For the Level 1 to Level 2 step, North Carolina requires 60 logged hours, including 10 at night. No more than 10 hours per week count; the Level 1 permit must also be held for 9 months.

The log and the calendar are separate requirements

DL-4A Driving Log — submitted with the Level 2 application. Use the NCDMV DL-4A log, with the supervising driver's signature. The Level 2 page accepts digital and printed copies. For the first 6 months, drive only from 5:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. with a supervising driver.

The weekly limit comes from G.S. 20-11(d)(5). Keep all practice entries but distinguish driven time from credited time. Extra time in a busy week cannot be moved to an empty week.

Mistakes that overstate progress

  • Counting all hours in a week that exceeds 10 hours.
  • Finishing total time but missing the separate 10-hour night target.
  • Scheduling a Level 2 application before the 9-month holding period ends.
  • Presenting unsigned entries or treating this supporting sheet as the state's form.
