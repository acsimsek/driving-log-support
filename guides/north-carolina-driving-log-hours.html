North Carolina: supervised driving hours, explained

North Carolina is one of the few states with a weekly cap: no more than 10 hours of the 60 may be logged in any one week, and the Level 1 permit must be held for 9 months. Cramming hours in the last month does not work here by design.

The requirement at a glance

Supervised practice required60 hours
Of which at night10 hours
Permit must be held9 months
Weekly hours that countmax 10 h/week
Supervising adultlicensed 5+ years; Parent, grandparent, guardian, or a person they approve
Log copy accepteddigital or printed

Permit-stage limit: For the first 6 months, drive only from 5:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. with a supervising driver.

Also required in North Carolina

  • Complete an approved driver education course before receiving the Level 1 permit
  • Have no moving, seat belt, or mobile-phone violation convictions during the previous 6 months
