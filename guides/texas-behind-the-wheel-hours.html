Texas supervised driving hours

Verified against official Texas sources on 2026-07-31.

30supervised hours required
10of those hours at night
6 mopermit holding period
2 h/daymaximum that counts
The detail most Texas families miss: only two hours per day of practice count toward the 30, no matter how long the drive actually was. It is printed on TDLR Form DES150N itself (rev. December 2024) — an app or a paper log that counts a longer day at face value overstates your progress.

The paperwork

TDLR Form DES150N — Driver Education 30 Hour Behind the Wheel Log — The parent or guardian certifies the whole log and signs with their driver licence number; a student over 18 signs for themselves. Each entry also carries the supervising adult's signature and DL number.

State-specific details to preserve

  • Student's name
  • DL #
  • Date
  • Time (am/pm)
  • Daytime hours
  • Nighttime hours
  • Adult's signature and DL #
