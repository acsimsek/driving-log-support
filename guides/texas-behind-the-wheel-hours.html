How many supervised driving hours does Texas require?

Texas requires 30 hours of supervised practice, including 10 at night. On top of the total, at most 2 hours count on any one day and the default supervised period is 6 calendar months, starting at permit issue date. At least 20 hours must be daytime practice.

The detail most Texas families miss: only two hours per day of practice count toward the 30, no matter how long the drive actually was. It is printed on TDLR Form DES150N itself (rev. December 2024) — an app or a paper log that counts a longer day at face value overstates your progress.

Which paperwork does Texas want?

TDLR Form DES150N — Driver Education 30 Hour Behind the Wheel Log — The parent or guardian certifies the whole log and signs with their driver licence number; a student over 18 signs for themselves. Each entry also carries the supervising adult's signature and DL number.

Common questions

How many driving hours do you need in Texas?

Texas requires 30 hours, including 10 at night. At least 20 hours must be in daytime.

Can you log all the hours in a few long days in Texas?

No. Texas credits at most 2 hours per day, so a longer drive still counts as that maximum. Spreading practice out is the only way to reach the total.

How long must the permit be held in Texas?

The default period is 6 calendar months, starting at permit issue date. Check the applicant and licence-stage paths below for exceptions.
