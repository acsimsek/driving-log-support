Virginia 45-hour driving log: 15 after sunset and who signs

For the teen learner-permit path. Rule data verified 2026-09-21; linked paperwork checked 17 October 2026.

45supervised hours required

9 calendar monthsdefault supervised period
Virginia teens need 45 hours of supervised driving, at least 15 of them after sunset, and must hold the learner's permit for 9 months (or until 18). A parent, foster parent or legal guardian certifies the hours; the signed 45-hour log goes to the in-car driver education instructor, not to DMV.

Where the 45 hours are certified

The Department of Education's 45-Hour Parent/Teen Driving Guide contains the log: date, skills, day and night minutes, and the parent's initials, with a certification block at the end that the parent signs with their DMV driver's license or ID number. The completed log goes to the in-car instructor and must be finished before the 180-day temporary license is issued.

The parent also signs the driver education completion certificate (DTS B or TDL-180), which certifies the same 45 hours. DMV accepts that certificate only from the driver training school, never directly from families. Neither document mentions a notary.

Who can supervise

Under Code of Virginia § 46.2-335: any licensed driver aged 21 or older, a parent or legal guardian, or a brother or sister (including half- and step-siblings) aged 18 or older. The supervisor sits beside the driver and must be alert and able to help. The person who supervises a drive does not have to be the parent who signs the certification.

Driver education alongside the log

The state program has 36 classroom sessions, seven driving sessions, seven observation sessions and a final road skills test, each session 50 minutes. It includes a 90-minute parent and teen component; in Northern Virginia (Planning District 8) a parent must attend it in person. The license is issued at 16 years and 3 months at the earliest.

Mistakes that hold up the temporary license

  • Not finishing the log before the in-car instructor needs it for the 180-day temporary license.
  • Reaching 45 hours with fewer than 15 after sunset.
  • Leaving the parent's DMV license or ID number off the certification.
  • Mailing the completion certificate to DMV yourself; only the school can submit it.
  • Assuming the January 2027 changes apply to teens. DMV says the rules for applicants under 18 stay the same; the changes cover ages 18 to 20.
