How many supervised driving hours does Virginia require?

Virginia requires 45 hours of supervised practice, including 15 at night. On top of the total, the permit must be held for 270 days.

Common questions

How many driving hours do you need in Virginia?

What counts as night driving in Virginia?

Virginia defines it as after sunset. Record the day and night parts of a drive separately as you go; splitting them afterwards is guesswork.

How long must the permit be held in Virginia?

At least 270 days, counted from the date the permit was issued.
