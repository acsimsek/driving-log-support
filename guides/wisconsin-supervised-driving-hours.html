Wisconsin supervised driving hours

Verified against official Wisconsin sources on 2026-07-31.

50supervised hours required
10of those hours at night
Wisconsin's HS-303 log is provided "for your convenience" — the binding step is the parent certification of 50 hours (10 at night). A quirk worth knowing: an hour with a qualified instructor may count as two, up to five actual instructor hours.
