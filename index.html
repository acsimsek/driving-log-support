How it works

Three steps, no setup. The first drive can be logged within a minute of installing.

Pick your state

Driving Log loads the hour target, night requirement and permit rules for your state, each cited to its official source.

Log each drive

Start the timer or add a completed drive by hand. Day and night minutes stay separate, and an interrupted timer can be recovered.

See what counts

Progress shows the time you drove next to the time your state actually credits — with the reason whenever they differ.

Know what counts before the DMV tells you

Some states cap how many hours count per day or per week. Others need a minimum number of practice days, or hours in particular conditions. Driving Log applies those rules as you go, so the total you see is the total that will hold up.

  • Daily and weekly caps applied automatically
  • Night hours tracked against your state's night target
  • Permit holding period counted from the date it was issued
  • Every rule linked to the official state page it came from

Common questions

Is the printable record an official DMV form?

No, and it says so on every page it prints. It is a dated supporting record of your drives. Some states accept a log like this; others require their own form — each state guide tells you which, with a link to the official document.

Does it need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Your data lives on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage that the developer cannot see.

Can I move my log from another app?

If the other app can export a CSV file, Pro can import it. You see a preview first, nothing is added until you confirm, and rows that can't be read are listed with the reason. See the comparison with RoadReady.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Every state guide has a one-click email to join the waitlist; we'll send one message if it ships and nothing else.
