Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

The short version. Driving Log has no advertising, no tracking, no developer account and no third-party analytics. Your records stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage that the developer cannot access.

Information you enter

The app can store a learner name, jurisdiction, permit date, license-stage and training answers, drive dates and times, day and night minutes, driving conditions, practiced skills, supervisor names, and edit history. This information is used only to provide the app's logging, progress, backup, restore, and document features.

Where information is stored

Records are stored locally in the app's protected container. When the device has an available iCloud account, Apple's private CloudKit service may mirror the records to that user's private iCloud database and to other devices signed in to the same account. The developer does not operate a server that receives these records and cannot inspect the user's private CloudKit content.

Backups and shared files

The app keeps up to 14 automatic recovery points in its local container. You can also create a readable JSON backup or a PDF and send it through the standard iOS share sheet. Those exported files are not encrypted by Driving Log; their destination and retention are controlled by you and the service you choose.

Retention and deletion

Removed drives remain in the app's Trash for up to 30 days unless restored or deleted sooner. Other records remain until you edit or delete them, remove the app's data, or manage the app's iCloud data through Apple settings. Files exported through the share sheet remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.

This website

This website has no forms, no analytics and no cookies. If you email us to join the Android waitlist, we keep your address only to send a single notification if an Android version ships. It is never shared or added to any other list, and it is deleted on request — reply "remove" at any time.

External links

The app and this site contain links to official government sources. Opening a link leaves the app or site and is governed by the destination website's privacy practices.

Children

Driving Log is a record-keeping tool for learner drivers and supervising adults. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information for the developer from children.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated.
