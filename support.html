Support

Email support@acsimsek.com for help, feedback or a feature request. Include your device model, iOS version, the Driving Log version, and whether iCloud was enabled. Please don't attach a backup file unless we ask — backups contain personal driving information.

A timer is still running after relaunch

Open Driving Log and use End Drive. If the timer has been running unusually long, choose the real end time shown by the recovery controls. The app never silently guesses forgotten driving time.

iCloud says "Waiting to back up"

Open Profile → iCloud sync details. Check that the device is signed in to iCloud, iCloud Drive is enabled, and there is a network connection and available iCloud storage. Local logging keeps working while sync is waiting.

Restore or move a log to a new phone

Open Profile → Backup and restore to save a JSON backup. On a fresh installation, choose Restore a backup before creating a new profile. A restore only adds what is missing; it never overwrites your current profile answers.

Recover a removed drive

Open Profile → Trash. Removed drives can be restored for 30 days unless they were permanently deleted.

Is the printed record an official form?

No. Driving Log is independent and is not affiliated with or approved by any DMV or licensing authority. Its documents are supporting records, clearly labelled as such. Follow the official source links in the app and in the state guides, and confirm current requirements with your licensing authority.
